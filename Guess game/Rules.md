# 🎯 Guess the Number

A simple **Java console game** where you try to guess the number secretly generated by the computer.

The computer chooses a random number between **1 and 100**, and you keep guessing until you find it. 🔢

## 🎮 How to Play

1. The computer generates a random number between **1 and 100**.
2. Enter your guess.
3. The game gives you a hint based on how close your guess is.
4. Keep guessing until you find the secret number. 🎯

### 🔥❄️ Rules

* ❄️ **Cold** → Your guess is more than 10 away from the secret number.
* 🔥 **Hot** → Your guess is within 10 of the secret number.
* 🎉 **Right** → Your guess matches the secret number.
* 🔁 **Unlimited attempts** → Keep guessing until you get it right!

### Example

```text
Secret Number = 50

Guess: 25
❄️ Cold

Guess: 43
🔥 Hot

Guess: 50
🎉 You guessed it Right!
```
